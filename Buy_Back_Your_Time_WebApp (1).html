75 Items to Delegate

The practice audit: who does the work now, and what AI can take off your plate.
You might be asking whether it’s worth the time to audit these tasks. Here’s the payoff: every hour you spend on work that AI or your team could handle is an hour you could buy back, for clients, for planning, or for your life. This sheet finds those hours, and shows you what they’re costing you today.

How to use this sheet

  1. For every task, check who does it TODAY: Owner, Advisor, Staff, AI, or N/A.
  2. Read the AI-fit tag: light green = AI can own it, medium = AI assists you, dark = a person must do it.
  3. In the last column, note the time you personally spend on it each week (hours and minutes). Enter your time on every task you handle, and we’ll show you which ones you could hand off.
  4. When you’re done, the Buy Back Your Time page adds it up to show how much time you could buy back, and what it’s costing you today.
